About ClusterTriage

Deep experience.
Narrow focus.

ClusterTriage is built around one specialist problem space: Microsoft failover-cluster infrastructure. The proposition is intentionally narrower than a general infrastructure consultancy because the value comes from repeatable depth.

Specialists should be able to show their method.

ClusterTriage combines long-term Microsoft infrastructure experience with a repeatable measurement and reporting method. The aim is not to produce the largest possible report, but to establish what the environment is actually doing, what matters operationally and whether remediation changed the measured state.

The same philosophy runs through the annual Assurance service and the incident-focused ClusterDown entry point: evidence first, judgement second, recommendations that can be verified.
