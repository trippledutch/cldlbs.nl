Hans VredevoortServer & Cluster Engineering & Consultancy

40+ years of experience in the Microsoft world. Participated in many infrastructure projects from SMB to Enterprise customers. Microsoft MVP for over a decade across the Failover Clustering, Virtual Machine and Cloud & Datacenter Management categories.

Co-author of Microsoft Private Cloud Computing: Building the Foundation , technical editor on Mastering Hyper-V Deployment , contributor and reviewer on the Windows Server 2012 Hyper-V Installation and Configuration Guide . Co-Founder of Hyper-V.nu (retired), the long-running community resource for Microsoft virtualization in the Benelux. Co-host of the Hyper-V Amigos showcast with peers across Europe.

CloudLabs is the consultancy practice behind that body of work. The focus is narrow on purpose: Hyper-V, Failover Clustering, Storage Spaces Direct, Azure Local, System Center VMM. Customers come for the depth, stay because the recommendations are actionable, and the report doesn't pretend the environment is fine when it isn't.
