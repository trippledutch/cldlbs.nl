Pricing

The HealthCheck comes in four forms.

They differ in rhythm, in what is delivered and in how long you commit.

FormWhat you getPrice
C1-yearAnnual subscription The standard form: a base measurement and three quarterly follow-ups, each with report and a one-hour review. 5,000 eurosper year
C1-quarterQuarterly subscription The same four measurements with report and review, invoiced per quarter; you do not commit for a year. 1,400 eurosper quarter
C1-monthMonthly subscription A monthly quick check whose outcome is the updated risk list; no written report and no review. 300 eurosper month
B1One-off base measurement One base measurement with report and review, without follow-ups, with the right to move up to the quarterly or annual subscription. 1,500 eurosone-off

The price follows the degree of commitment. Per measurement with report and review you pay 1,250 euros in the annual subscription, 1,400 euros in the quarterly subscription (5,600 euros on a yearly basis) and 1,500 euros for the one-off base measurement: the same work, with a premium for the freedom to stop after any measurement. If you move to a subscription after the one-off base measurement, that measurement is the start of the cycle.

The monthly subscription (3,600 euros per year) is not a cheaper version of the same thing but a different delivery: a measurement every month, whose outcome is the updated risk list. That suits a team that handles the follow-up itself and mainly wants a regularly measured signal; anyone who also wants the report and the review picks one of the other forms.
