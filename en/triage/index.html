What the triage list is, and is not

What you get

Five signals, sorted by severity, each with the measured value, the node and the timestamp. Whatever could not be read is listed too, with the reason.

What it is not

No cause and no reconstruction: triage sorts, the diagnosis is the work of R1. No report, no review session, and no subscription attached.

More than five found?The triage list also states the total number of signals found. The full risk list, sorted by severity, is available on request as a paid intermediate step; the five heaviest are always in the free list.

Safe and read-only

  • Nothing is installed and nothing is changed; nothing is left behind afterwards.
  • No passwords are processed or stored. Before the file is written, the script checks its own output for values that look like credentials.
  • Memory dumps do not travel in the free variant.
  • What you hand over is used solely to produce the triage list.

If the list points at something

R1, the root cause investigation.The reconstruction across all nodes, with timeline, root cause analysis and RCA document, backed by deep log and dump analysis. Orderable on its own.

B1, the one-off base measurement. The complete picture of the cluster, with findings report, a one-hour review and the right to move up to a subscription. Its big brother is C1: the full cycle with three quarterly follow-up measurements.

Both are listed with their prices on the HealthCheck page. Ordered together they form the package deal Incident investigation complete (R1 + B1), at a promo price on request.
