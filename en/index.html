What Triage is and is not

You get

  • The five heaviest signals, sorted by severity
  • Measured values, nodes involved and time
  • Parts that were not read too, with the reason
  • Direction for the safest next step
  • A free first assessment without a subscription

You do not yet get

  • No definitive cause or full reconstruction
  • No extensive RCA document
  • No full HealthCheck report
  • No repair execution or configuration changes
  • No guaranteed response time or emergency line

Safe and read-only

Nothing is installed or changed. Passwords are not processed or stored. Memory dumps are not included in the free variant. Your data is used solely to produce the triage list.
