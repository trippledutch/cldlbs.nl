General terms and conditions

These terms apply to every CloudLabs engagement. They describe what you may expect from us, what we need from you and where the limits lie. Last changed: September 2026.

1. Definitions

  1. CloudLabs: the sole proprietorship CloudLabs, trading under that name, also referred to in these terms as we or us.
  2. Customer: the party entering into an agreement with CloudLabs, also referred to in these terms as you.
  3. Engagement: the agreed service, in one of the forms set out in article 4.
  4. Measurement: the read-only reading of a cluster and its surrounding environment with the CloudLabs scripts.
  5. Report: the findings report and the other documents that result from a measurement.
  6. Cycle: a period of twelve months with a base measurement and three follow-up measurements.

2. Applicability

  1. These terms apply to every quotation, engagement and agreement between CloudLabs and the customer, and to everything arising from them.
  2. The customer's own terms do not apply, unless CloudLabs has accepted them in writing in advance.
  3. Where an arrangement in the engagement confirmation differs from these terms, that arrangement prevails.

3. Quotation and formation

  1. A quotation is valid for thirty days, unless it states another term.
  2. The agreement is formed when the customer accepts the quotation or engagement confirmation in writing or by email.
  3. Prices and descriptions on the website are indicative. What the quotation states prevails.

4. Forms of the engagement

  1. The HealthCheck comes in four forms: the annual subscription (C1-year), the quarterly subscription (C1-quarter), the monthly subscription (C1-month) and the one-off base measurement (B1). What each form covers and what it costs is on the rates page.
  2. Modules extend the measurement and run along in every measurement of the cycle in which they were purchased.
  3. Incident investigation (R1) is orderable separately, without a subscription too.
  4. An engagement concerns one cluster configuration. Several clusters at the same customer are several engagements.

5. Prices and VAT

  1. All amounts are in euros and exclude VAT.
  2. VAT is charged where it is due. For business customers within the EU outside the Netherlands the VAT is reverse charged where applicable, provided the customer supplies a valid VAT identification number.
  3. CloudLabs may adjust its rates annually. An adjustment does not take effect during a running cycle.

6. Invoicing and payment

  1. The payment term is thirty days from the invoice date.
  2. The annual subscription is invoiced at the start of the cycle, the quarterly subscription quarterly in advance, the monthly subscription monthly in advance and the one-off base measurement on engagement.
  3. If payment is not made, CloudLabs may suspend performance after giving the customer written notice of default and a period of fourteen days.
  4. In case of late payment, statutory commercial interest is due and the reasonable costs of collection are for the customer's account.

7. Performance and cooperation

  1. CloudLabs performs the engagement to the best of its insight and ability, as an obligation of effort. No result is guaranteed.
  2. The customer provides on time and correctly what is needed: access, points of contact, and the data requested at the intake.
  3. If a part is not supplied or not released, it is not measured and that is stated as an explicit limitation in the report.
  4. CloudLabs works remotely. Work on site is possible where useful; travel and accommodation costs are invoiced at cost.
  5. There is no on-call service and no response time is guaranteed.

8. Access, security and the read-only nature of the measurement

  1. The measurement is read-only. Nothing is installed, nothing is changed and nothing is restarted, and nothing is left behind afterwards.
  2. The scripts are run by the customer or visibly under supervision, in a session the customer watches. The customer therefore keeps control at all times.
  3. CloudLabs receives no passwords and does not ask for them. Read rights are sufficient; which ones is recorded at the intake.
  4. Before a script runs, it shows the plan for the run and asks for confirmation.

9. Delivery and languages

  1. Delivery consists of a customer-facing report in an editable format, in the standard CloudLabs layout.
  2. On follow-up measurements a delta report and a remaining-risk report with a risk trend are also delivered.
  3. As-Built system documentation and a Root Cause Analysis are orderable separately.
  4. The language of the reporting is fixed at the intake and applies to the whole engagement.
  5. If the customer wants to publish the report in their own house style or that of their customer, they apply that layout themselves. CloudLabs delivers the content.

10. Repair work and additional work

  1. Repair work is included in none of the forms. The customer chooses whether to carry it out, give it to their own supplier or ask CloudLabs.
  2. If CloudLabs carries out repair work, it does so on an hourly basis at the applicable rate, and always in a session the customer watches and in which the actions are performed or approved on the customer's side.
  3. Work outside the agreed scope is discussed in advance and confirmed separately.

11. Confidentiality

  1. A non-disclosure agreement is signed as standard before work starts.
  2. Both parties keep confidential what they learn from each other and of which they know, or should know, that it is confidential.
  3. The measurement data and the inventory output remain the property of the customer. CloudLabs does not keep them beyond the term of the engagement, unless agreed otherwise.
  4. CloudLabs may publish about an engagement in anonymised form: sector, size, environment type and measured values, without customer name, cluster name, host names, IP addresses or exact dates. The customer may object to this in advance.

12. Personal data

  1. CloudLabs processes as little personal data as possible. The measurement is aimed at systems and configuration, not at people.
  2. The privacy statement applies to the processing of personal data.
  3. Where CloudLabs processes personal data on behalf of the customer in an engagement, the parties conclude a data processing agreement for it.

13. Intellectual property

  1. The scripts, the toolchain, the templates and the method of CloudLabs remain the property of CloudLabs.
  2. The customer receives a perpetual, non-exclusive right of use on the delivered report for their own use, including the right to provide it internally and to their own customer.
  3. The customer may not publish the report in a way that makes the CloudLabs method as a whole reproducible by third parties.

14. Time frames

  1. Stated time frames are indicative and not strict deadlines, unless expressly agreed otherwise.
  2. If a delivery is delayed by circumstances on the customer's side, the planning shifts accordingly.

15. Liability

  1. CloudLabs delivers an assessment based on what was readable at the time of measuring. The customer remains responsible for managing their environment and for the decision whether or not to follow a recommendation.
  2. The liability of CloudLabs is limited, per event and per year, to the amount invoiced for the engagement concerned in the preceding twelve months, excluding VAT.
  3. CloudLabs is not liable for indirect damage, including lost profit, missed savings, business interruption, loss of data and damage from third-party claims.
  4. If the customer or a third party carries out a change, CloudLabs is not liable for its consequences, not even where the change rests on a CloudLabs recommendation.
  5. The limitations in this article do not apply in case of intent or wilful recklessness on the part of CloudLabs.
  6. A claim lapses twelve months after the customer discovered the damage or could reasonably have discovered it.

16. Force majeure

  1. In case of force majeure the obligations are suspended for as long as the force majeure lasts.
  2. Force majeure includes in any case: failures at suppliers, outages of networks or cloud services, government measures, and illness of the person performing the engagement, given that CloudLabs is a sole proprietorship.
  3. If the force majeure lasts longer than sixty days, either party may terminate the agreement in writing for the part not yet performed, without becoming liable for damages.

17. Term, termination and renewal

  1. The annual subscription runs for twelve months and is renewed tacitly by twelve months, unless either party gives written notice at the latest one month before the end.
  2. The quarterly and monthly subscriptions run per quarter and per month respectively and can be terminated with effect from the end of the current period.
  3. The one-off base measurement ends with the delivery of the report and the review.
  4. On interim termination by the customer, what has already been performed remains payable; periods already invoiced are not refunded.

18. Complaints

  1. The customer reports a complaint about what was delivered within thirty days of delivery, in writing and with a description of what is wrong.
  2. If the complaint is justified, CloudLabs repairs the defect or delivers again. If repair is not possible, the amount invoiced for that part is credited.

19. Changes to these terms

  1. CloudLabs may change these terms. For a running engagement the terms as they read when it was entered into apply.
  2. The date at the bottom of this page states when the terms were last changed.

20. Governing law and competent court

  1. Dutch law applies to all agreements.
  2. Disputes are submitted to the competent court in Amsterdam, unless mandatory law designates another court.
  3. The parties will make an effort to resolve matters together before submitting a dispute.
