What data we collect and why

Forms

The request and contact forms on this site send nothing themselves. What you fill in is opened in your own email client or WhatsApp, so that you send the message yourself. This site does not store what you entered and there is no server that receives it. We use what you send us solely to answer your question and, if you become a customer, to deliver the agreed service.

Legal basis: Legitimate interest (pre-contractual steps) and, where an engagement follows, performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

Cookies and measurement

This site sets no cookies of its own and does not measure your visit. No analytics software runs, there are no visitor statistics, and there is therefore no cookie banner either. What you do on this site is recorded nowhere.

Calendly

The appointment page carries an embedded Calendly calendar. It only loads when you open that page, and as soon as you interact with it Calendly places its own cookies and Calendly's terms and privacy policy apply. If you would rather not, give your preferred times through the form below it; that sends through your own email or WhatsApp.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • Calendly (if you book a call, subject to their own policy)
  • When legally required

Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. Contact us at hans.vredevoort@cldlbs.com to exercise these rights. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

Cookie settings

There is nothing to set: this site places no cookies and measures nothing. Only the embedded Calendly calendar places cookies, and only if you use it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page reflects the last change. Continued use of the site after an update does not constitute acceptance of new terms that require consent. We will show a new banner if that is the case.
